# An Open Letter to Developers

"When the river warms in spring the ducks know first — it’s the season when pufferfish start to rise!"

Dear friends — I’m John, an ordinary Java engineer working in a company. Today I write to you with a sincere heart to reach out to everyone who loves AI and innovation.

Six months ago I discovered many excellent AI projects like Dify and Chat2DB. I thought how wonderful it would be to participate in them — yet opportunities felt out of reach and I kept writing code for years without the chance.

Earlier this year I watched a video by the Xiage team and grew curious about how they built their system. I wanted to reproduce a backend service and create a low-cost, civilian-friendly Jarvis. The current result is still rough — low concurrency, slow responses, lots of bugs — more of a clumsy assistant than the dream I pictured.

The Xiage team is our inspiration, and I understand why they chose not to open-source everything. Still, I believe in the proverb: "One flower alone does not make a spring; when a hundred flowers bloom, spring fills the garden." We live in an age when AI is blooming — perhaps this generation will realize wide, practical AI. With our own hands we can build an affordable, civilian Jarvis. If they can do it, so can we — it’s only a matter of time. I call this our "journey to seek the scriptures."

What difficulties will we face on this journey? I expect at least eighty-one challenges. There will be monsters and setbacks, but also helpers and fellow travelers who join our cause.

If you find my words amusing, then I’m fortunate — being able to make you smile for five seconds out of your roughly 30,000 days on Earth feels like a small contribution.

Will the idea of a low-cost, civilian Jarvis fail? I don’t know. But ordinary lives are full of attempts that didn’t succeed — that’s part of being human.

One thing I’m confident about: sooner or later someone will fully reproduce the features of the Xiage team and create a practical, low-cost Jarvis for the public. Will this project be that someone? I certainly hope so.

I look forward to walking this path with you and building the future together.

— John, 2025-03-11, Guangzhou

# Appendix — Contributor Guide

## Project goals

1. Practical, low-cost Jarvis-like solution for civilian use.
2. Intelligent integration with nearby hardware and devices.

## Join us

We warmly welcome like-minded friends to contribute. See our short-term roadmap and issue list here: https://github.com/users/xinnan-tech/projects/3 — items without an assigned owner are good opportunities to help out.

Ways to participate:

### 1) Become a contributor

Fork the repository and submit PRs. After review by maintainers, PRs will be merged into the main branch.

### 2) Become a developer

After you have 3 accepted PRs, contact the maintainer to request developer access. The maintainer will invite you to the developers’ chat to discuss the project's direction.

## Developer workflow

1. Create a feature branch for each task. Use a short, descriptive branch name so it's clear what you are building and to avoid collisions.
2. Submit a PR for review. When the PR is approved by other developers, it will be merged into the main branch.
